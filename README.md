

## About

What if something as simple as baking a cake could bring people together for something bigger? ❤️

Over the last weekend of November 2026, we'll be gathering virtually for our Namoura Cook-Along Workshop, where food becomes a way to connect, learn and stand together in solidarity with Palestine.

Across generations, recipes have carried stories of home, family, culture and belonging. They remind us that preserving traditions is also a way of keeping memories and identities alive.

And that's what makes this gathering special. 🌿

It's not just about learning to make a delicious, syrup-soaked semolina cake. It's about creating space for conversations, building connections across communities and finding meaningful ways to keep showing up for Palestine.

Because solidarity doesn't always begin with grand gestures. Sometimes, it begins with something as simple as coming together. ❤️

Come for the baking, stay for the conversations and community. 🇵🇸

🍰 Ready to register? Please make a donation to Gaza Soup Kitchen via [every.org](https://bit.ly/luna4gaza]), a platform that charges zero administrative fees.
